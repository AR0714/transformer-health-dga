Section: IEEE C57.104-2019 Section 4 — Action Levels for Dissolved Gas Analysis
IEEE C57.104 defines four action levels based on gas concentrations in ppm:
Action Level 1 (normal): H2 < 100, CH4 < 75, C2H6 < 75, C2H4 < 75, C2H2 < 3, CO < 500.
Continue normal operation and monitoring schedule.
Action Level 2 (attention): concentrations 2 to 4 times Level 1 limits.
Increase monitoring frequency. Investigate cause. No immediate shutdown required.
Action Level 3 (high concern): concentrations 4 to 8 times Level 1 limits.
Reduce loading if possible. Plan maintenance. Re-sample within one week.
Action Level 4 (critical): concentrations exceed 8 times Level 1 limits or rapid gas generation.
Remove from service if possible. Risk of failure is significant. Immediate investigation required.
C2H2 above 35 ppm always indicates serious arcing regardless of other gas levels.
